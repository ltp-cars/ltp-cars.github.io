Questions people ask before booking

What is the difference between a taxi and a minicab?

In London a black cab can be hailed on the street and runs a meter. A minicab, like ours, is private hire: always pre-booked, with the price agreed first. Both are licensed by TfL.

My flight is delayed. Do I pay more?

No. We watch your flight and move the pickup time to match when you actually land, at no extra charge.

Where do I find the driver at the airport?

In the arrivals hall, holding a board with your name. They will help carry your bags to the car.

Can you fit a child seat?

Yes. Add infant, toddler or booster seats when you book. Each seat costs £15 and is fitted before we collect you.

How early should I book?

For airport runs and early mornings, a day ahead is safest. Same-day bookings are fine when a driver is free.

How do I pay?

By card when you book online, or by card or cash to the driver.
